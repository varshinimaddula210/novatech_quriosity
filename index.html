Basis Switching

A qubit is hiding a secret.
Ask it the right question to reveal it.
Ask the wrong one — and it forgets.

How to Play

🎯
Your goal: Reveal the qubit's secret value (0 or 1) by measuring it in the correct basis.
🧭
Two bases: The Z-basis reads vertical spin (↑↓). The X-basis reads horizontal spin (←→). Each qubit is prepared in one of them.
✅
Right basis: You get the true value — 0 or 1. The secret is revealed.
💥
Wrong basis: The qubit collapses into a random state. You get noise, not signal — and lose a life. This is measurement scrambling.
🔍
Clues: Each round gives you a context clue. Read it carefully — it hints at which basis the secret is encoded in.
