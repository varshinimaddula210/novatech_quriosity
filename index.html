How to Play

🦶
Read the footprints. Footprints going up-down mean the thief moves vertically. Footprints going left-right mean horizontal movement.
🧭
Pick your basis. Z-basis scans a full row (vertical read). X-basis scans a full column (horizontal read).
🔦
Choose a line. Click a row number (Z) or column number (X) to scan it. If the thief is there — caught!
💥
Wrong basis? The thief's quantum state collapses. They teleport to a random cell, footprints scramble, and you lose a scan.
⚡
You have 5 scans. Use them wisely. Run out and the thief escapes.
