# Our Approach — Quriosity 2026

**Topic:** Basis Switching and Measurement Scrambling
**Team size:** 4

---

## The Philosophy

Explore first. Build around confusion, not confidence.
The game should fix the exact moment things stopped making sense — not explain what we already understood.

---

## Exploration Strategy

### Stage 1 — Two people go in blind (≈ 1 hour)
- A + B explore the topic independently
- Use the qubit demo on the event site, articles, videos — anything
- Try to *predict* outcomes before measuring, note every wrong prediction
- Do not wait to "fully get it" before moving to stage 2
- **Write down every point where intuition breaks**

### Stage 2 — Explain it to fresh ears (≈ 45 min)
- A + B explain basis switching to C + D, who have never heard of it
- C + D ask "but why?" aggressively — no question is too basic
- A + B explain the moment they *think* they get it, not when they're sure
- The holes in the explanation are the data
- **Scribe notes every new question that comes up**

### Stage 3 — Pick the one mechanic (≈ 15 min)
- All 4 sit together
- Review the full confusion list from both stages
- Vote on the single confusion point with the most natural game mechanic hiding inside it
- Everything else gets cut

**Target: start building by hour 2.5**

## Known Beginner Confusion Points (pre-identified)

These are the walls most beginners hit with basis switching. Our game should address at least one of these directly:

- Why are there multiple bases at all?
- Why does measuring collapse the state?
- Why can't you just measure in both bases?
- What's actually random vs what's determined?
- Why does asking the "wrong question" destroy the answer to the right one?
